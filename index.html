About MediChain

A hospital record that outlives the hospital that wrote it.

In an ordinary hospital system, medical records sit in a database the hospital itself controls. Anyone with the right access can change a diagnosis, backdate an entry, or remove one — and leave no trace behind.

MediChain moves those records onto the Ethereum blockchain. Each entry is signed by the wallet of the doctor who wrote it, timestamped by the network, and kept where no single party can revise it. Correcting a record adds a new version; voiding one marks it closed. Neither deletes what came before.

Each record carries the wallet signature of the doctor who wrote it, so authorship can be proven rather than trusted.

Access decided by contract

Admins register staff, doctors write records, patients read their own. Those rules live in the smart contract, where the interface cannot bypass them.

Nothing disappears

Deleting a record marks it void. The original entry stays on-chain, where an auditor can still find it years later.

The flow

How a record is made

Three wallets, three jobs. No one holds more access than their role allows.

  1. 1

    The hospital admits a wallet

    An admin registers a doctor or a patient by their wallet address, and stores the name and contact details off-chain.

  2. 2

    A doctor writes the entry

    Diagnosis and treatment are signed in MetaMask and committed to the chain, stamped with the time and the author.

  3. 3

    The patient reads it back

    Patients open their own chart with the same wallet they were registered with. Read-only, always.

Permissions

Three roles, one portal

What you see after connecting depends entirely on which wallet you hold.

Admin

Administrator

Registers doctors and patients, keeps the hospital directory, and removes access when someone leaves.

Clinical

Doctor

Admits patients, writes and revises medical records, and voids entries they authored — but never another doctor's.

Read-only

Patient

Reads their own medical history and keeps their contact details current. Cannot write to the chain.
